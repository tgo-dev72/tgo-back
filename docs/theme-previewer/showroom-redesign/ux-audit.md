# UX audit

The previous preview shell exposed a technical dropdown, duplicated labels, weak grouping, excess canvas margins and no distinction between a reusable theme and a curated visual preset. Beauty also rendered its hero fallback because demo product media was synthetic SVG art rather than photography.

The redesign establishes three toolbar zones: theme identity/navigation, real viewport controls, and discovery/actions. The storefront remains the visual priority. Theme discovery and preset selection share one compact popover, loading preserves frame geometry, and device buttons use actual widths rather than transforms.

Public demo writes, checkout, payments and tenant membership remain blocked. The standalone action opens another showroom route rather than revealing an internal repository or credential.
